Tap a number to type it in. With a keyboard, use the ↑ and ↓ arrow keys to change a count, and hold Shift to add or remove 5 notes, 10 coins or 5 rolls at a time. Each coin also has a counter for bank rolls of that coin (the value of a full roll is shown next to it), and its line total covers loose coins and rolls together. In Safe mode only coin rolls are counted, as the safe holds no loose coins. Counts are cleared when you close the app; the float and expected safe amounts are remembered.
